# OUTBREAK FESTIVAL

*a Spooky1611 DiRT 3 modification*

DiRT 3's **Outbreak** party mode (car infection tag in an arena), rebuilt from scratch as its own
game. One car starts out infected, and everyone it hits turns green and joins the hunt. Survivors
run and hide until the clock runs out. Play it against bots or online with friends.

**[Download the newest build](../../releases/latest)**: the `Outbreak-Festival-build-N.zip` under *Assets*.

> You need your own copy of **DiRT 3 Complete Edition** (Steam). No part of DiRT 3 comes with
> this download. The first time you start the game, it builds itself on your PC from **your**
> copy.

---

## What's in it

- **Outbreak, the way DiRT 3 played it.** The scatter, the random first infection, the clock and
  the rounds, with DiRT's own announcer, read from your install.
- **Battersea**, the power station arena, with its ramps, decks, the train yard and the river.
- **The Gymkhana cars**, driving with DiRT 3's own handling. Each car's handling data is decoded from
  your copy by running the game's own loader, and runs in a new physics engine.
- **Bots** that hunt, cut you off, hide round corners and side-step a hunter coming in fast.
- **Online play.** Host a room, or join from the server list or with a code. Everyone has to be
  on the same build, and the launcher keeps you there.
- **Free roam**, to drive around the arena on your own.
- **Replays and the killcam**: the last catch from the hunter's view, with full-match replays and
  TV cameras.
- **Crashes and damage**: panels crumple, come loose and tear off, with sparks, glass and smoke.
- **Match rules** for the host to set, and a HUD set to **CASUAL**, **NORMAL** or **HARDCORE**.
- **Keyboard, mouse or gamepad**: Xbox, PlayStation (DualShock 3/4, DualSense), Switch Pro and Steam
  controllers, with rumble. Steam doesn't have to be running.

## Where it's at: alpha

This is the start. The first public builds are an **alpha**: the skeleton of the core game is
here and playable (the Outbreak rules, the physics, the bots, online play, the damage and one
arena), so we have a solid base to build on. From here it grows: **more maps**, **better
physics**, **more modifiers** to change up a match, and more besides. Expect rough edges along
the way, and expect each build to add something.

## Install

1. Download `Outbreak-Festival-build-N.zip` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Unzip it somewhere you can write to (Documents, Desktop, `D:\Games`), **not** Program Files.
3. Start **Outbreak Festival.exe**.
4. The launcher finds DiRT 3 by itself. If it can't, click **Browse...** and pick the folder that
   has `dirt3_game.exe` in it.
5. Press **INSTALL & PLAY**. The first time, it converts your DiRT 3 for the game. That takes
   a few minutes and needs about 1.5 GB. After that it's just **PLAY**.

Windows SmartScreen may say *"Windows protected your PC"*, because the game isn't signed with a
paid certificate. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Updates

Install once and the launcher handles the rest. Every time you start it, it checks for a new
build and **downloads only the files that changed**. It then re-converts **only the parts of DiRT 3
that the update touched**, not the whole game again. Updates are signed, so the launcher only
accepts builds from this page.

New players don't have to install an old build and update it: every release has a **complete
zip** of that build.

## Questions

**Does this include any DiRT 3 files?**
No. The download has our own code and our own content: the launcher, the game, the converters, and
our own arena edits and lights. Everything that comes from DiRT 3 (cars, track, sounds, textures)
is made on your PC from your own copy. Please don't share your `data\` folder: everyone makes
their own.

**Something went wrong.**
Open an [issue](../../issues) or tell Spooky1611 what happened. The launcher's **Logs** button
opens the files that help.

**Online says we need the same update.**
Restart the launcher on both PCs. It updates to the newest build.
